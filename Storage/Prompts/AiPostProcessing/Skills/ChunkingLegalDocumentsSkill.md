# Skill: Chunking Legal and Regulatory Documents

Regulatory documents must be chunked by semantic legal units.

Rules:

1. A numbered article is usually one semantic chunk.
2. A numbered article with subpoints should remain one chunk if the subpoints are part of the same requirement, definition, list, or condition.
3. Definitions under one introductory point should remain together.
4. Principles listed under one point should remain together.
5. Contract elements listed under one point should remain together.
6. Do not split every subpoint into a separate chunk unless each subpoint is an independent article.
7. Do not place [chunk_end] after headings.
8. Do not place [chunk_end] between an introductory sentence and its list.
9. Place [chunk_end] after the complete legal unit.
